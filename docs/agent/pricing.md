# Guide and Operator Pricing

**Guide** and **Operator** are AI-powered assistants designed to help you create decks.

- **Guide** is an advisor. It answers questions, explains concepts, and can be a brainstorming partner. It never modifies your card, blueprints, or the scene.
- **Operator** makes edits for you. It can change blueprint logic, adjust scene layouts, modify actor behaviors, and more.

Together, they combine high level guidance with hands on creation.

## Pricing

Pricing is based on card size, measured in tokens. Most cards (around 80%) are **80k tokens or smaller** and are priced at:

- **4 bricks** per Guide request
- **12 bricks** per Operator request

Cards that exceed 80k tokens cost more:

- **7 bricks** per Guide request
- **21 bricks** per Operator request

For every extra 10k tokens above the 80k threshold, additional bricks are added:

- **+1 brick** for Guide
- **+3 bricks** for Operator
