Make a breakout game from scratch with a real game loop.

Requirements:
- Classic single-screen breakout with all of these working:
  - Touch/drag-controlled paddle that follows the touch x position.
  - A bouncing ball with believable wall/paddle/brick collisions and a bit of paddle-angle influence on bounce direction.
  - At least 4 rows of bricks across the top, ideally with a couple of colors.
  - Score that increments visibly on each brick break.
  - Lives counter visible on screen; lose a life when the ball drops past the paddle.
  - Win state when all bricks are cleared.
  - Game-over state when lives reach zero.
  - Brief visual feedback on brick break (e.g. brick disappears with a small flash, particle, or color change).
  - Animated motion: the ball moves smoothly each frame, not in big jumps.
- Show score, lives, and game-state text on screen with readable text sizes.
